# M2 Discovery

## What changed

- **Opening screen:** two destinations, "Find a cinema" (city, search and real cinemas with provider links) and "Explore the seat view" (opens the 3D seat preview).
- **Demo section:** the fictional schedule moved below, under a heading that starts with "Demo".
- **Phones:** on Movies, the city selector and search dock at the bottom of the screen. On the landing page, they sit directly under the title with the first real cinemas.
- **Back:** each history entry stores its scroll position and the last cinema clicked. Back waits for the content to load, then restores both, with focus on that cinema.
- **Returning later:** the last search is kept on this device for 14 days. Opening Movies for that city restores it, with a "Clear" link, and the landing page offers "Continue your search".
- **Outage fix:** the booking page no longer throws an uncaught error when the backend is unreachable. It shows "Can’t reach the booking server right now."

## Journeys tested

Playwright, Chromium, 1440×900 and 390×844, full backend on PostgreSQL. 13 passed, 1 skipped (the dock check runs at phone size only).

- Two destinations on the first screen, Demo kept apart, and "Explore the seat view" opens the preview.
- Journey A: Chennai, "Forum Mall", PVR Palazzo with the exact District link. The provider tab opens (stubbed), and the search is restored after leaving and returning.
- Thumb-reach dock at 390 px, with city switching and search.
- Back restores scroll within 40 px and focus on the cinema.
- Sports routes, event deep links, a seat deep link and the legacy `/?venue=` redirect.
- **Failure: slow API.** A 2.5 s delay shows "Loading Hyderabad…" and then the results.
- **Failure: backend outage.** The booking page shows a message and throws no uncaught error.

## Compared with M1

M1 on a phone led with the demo chart and showed no real cinema on the first screen. M2 shows three real Chennai cinemas and the search there.

## Remaining risks

- The docked search was checked in Chrome's phone emulation only. On real iOS Safari, the keyboard can cover a bottom-fixed field, which needs a device check.
- The restored search is per device, not per account.
