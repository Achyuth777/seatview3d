# M3 Seat preview

## What changed

- **New preview** (`/preview/:auditoriumId`): the 3D hall, a chart-paper detail sheet and a 2D seat map, using the Reservation Chart tokens on a dark stage.
- **One update:** a single reducer drives selection. Picking a seat highlights it on the map, retargets the camera and refreshes the row, seat number and measurements in the same render.
- **Interruptible camera:** each pose has a key, and only the latest pose may report that the camera has stopped. Three rapid picks end on the third, checked by both the label and the camera position.
- **Compare mode:** seat A stays put while you pick B. A and B switch the view, a table compares the measurements, and the URL keeps both seats (`?seat=K12&compare=C3`).
- **Phone layout:** the view sits on top, with a compact sheet below. On phones the seat map comes straight after the seat title, so the chosen seats stay visible, in compare mode too.
- **Reduced motion:** the camera switches instantly and the seat label fades gently.
- **Lazy 3D:** three.js and React Three load only on 3D routes. The landing page downloads about 99 KB of gzipped JavaScript instead of about 357 KB.
- **WebGL fallback:** without WebGL, the stage shows a section through the hall from the chosen seat. The seat map and measurements still work.
- **Keyboard:** the seat map has one tab stop, arrow keys move between seats, Enter or Space selects, and focus shows a red outline.

## Journeys tested

Playwright, Chromium, 1440×900 and 390×844. 19 passed, 1 skipped (the phone-sheet test runs at phone size only).

- Seat selection in one update, with the camera position checked against the seat's eye point to within 5 cm.
- Three rapid picks end on the last seat, with video recorded.
- Compare mode keeps seat A, switches between A and B, survives a reload and exits cleanly.
- At 390 px, the selected seat and both compared seats are visible on screen and not covered.
- Reduced motion: the camera arrives within 1.2 s of the click, with the gentle fade.
- Keyboard: Right then Up moves from K12 to J13, focus is visible, and Enter selects.
- Lazy loading: Home and Movies request no 3D code, and the preview requests it.
- WebGL switched off (Chromium flags): the 2D section updates with the seat, and no canvas is created.
- **Failure: slow 3D download.** A 4 s delay shows "Loading the 3D hall…", the seat map works meanwhile, and the camera catches up.
- **Failure: unknown auditorium.** It shows not found, never a generic map.
- Low-power profile (4× CPU): rapid picks still land on the last seat.

## What failed and how it was fixed

1. **Camera reported arrival before moving.** With motion off, the camera said it had arrived while still at the overview position, 33 m away. It now reports on the next frame, after the camera has actually moved.
2. **Compared seat hidden on phones.** The comparison table pushed the seat map below the screen. On phones the map now comes before the measurements.
3. **three.js still preloaded.** A forced "three" chunk group drew shared helpers into the entry chunk. Removing the group let the lazy routes split naturally.
